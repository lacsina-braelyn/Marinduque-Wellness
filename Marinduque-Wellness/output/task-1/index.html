Task #1: Sector Selection

Selected Sector

Health & Wellness Innovation

Local Challenge

Community members in Marinduque may benefit from having simple, accessible health and wellness information in one place. People need convenient ways to learn about healthy habits, care for their mental well-being, and track their daily wellness activities.

Target Users

Residents of Marinduque, including students, families, and other community members who want to develop healthier daily habits.

Proposed Project

Marinduque Wellness: Innovation in Health and Wellness

Marinduque Wellness is a simple, responsive website designed to promote healthy living in the community. It provides general wellness information and interactive tools that encourage users to take care of their physical and mental health.

Key Features

  1. Health and Wellness Guide — Provides general tips about nutrition, hydration, physical activity, rest, and mental well-being.
  2. Interactive Daily Wellness Checklist — Allows users to check completed healthy habits and monitor their daily progress.
  3. Health Support Information — Encourages users to consult qualified health professionals or local health centers when they have health concerns.

Expected Output

A mobile-friendly website that promotes health awareness and encourages the people of Marinduque to practice healthy habits through accessible information and an interactive daily checklist.
